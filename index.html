03 · Background

Experience

  1. Jul 2025 – present

    Data Analyst and AI Lead · Gunnison County Assessor's Office

    • Built four tools that office staff use every day: an AI photo processor that replaced manual photo sorting for about $0.002 a photo, a parcel review dashboard that republishes itself about two minutes after each data export, a weekly short-term-rental monitor ahead of the county’s 2027 licensing program, and a map platform where staff join any spreadsheet to parcels without GIS software.
